Privacy

This page explains what personal data Taragot Sounds collects through this website, what we use it for, and what rights you have.

Who is responsible

Taragot Sounds is responsible for the personal data collected on this website. You can contact us at post@taragot.no or at Skedsmogata 5, 0655 Oslo, Norway.

The newsletter

When you subscribe to the newsletter, we store your name and email address. We only use them to send you news about our artists and releases. The legal basis is your consent, which you give when you subscribe.

The data is stored with Brevo, the service we use to send newsletters. Brevo processes the data on our behalf and stores it in the EU. We do not share it with anyone else.

We keep the data until you unsubscribe. You can unsubscribe at any time using the link at the bottom of every newsletter, or by sending us an email.

When you visit the website

We do not use cookies or any tools to track or analyse visits.

The website is hosted by GitHub Pages, which records your IP address in technical logs to keep the service secure. Some of the album covers are shown directly from Bandcamp, which means Bandcamp also receives your IP address.

When you follow a link to Bandcamp, Instagram or Facebook, that service’s privacy policy applies.

Your rights

You have the right to know what data we hold about you, and to have it corrected or deleted. You can also withdraw your consent at any time. Send us an email at post@taragot.no and we will help you.

If you believe we are handling your data in breach of the rules, you can complain to the Norwegian Data Protection Authority, Datatilsynet ↗ (opens in a new tab).
